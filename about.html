02 / 06 — About

People who switch on a light with warmth

There is no shortage of good work and good people, yet only some of them are ever seen. ONUA came together to light up the rest.

The name

What the name means

온 溫

Warmth

The Korean syllable 온 carries the warmth of a heated room, or a bowl of rice kept aside for someone.

ON

Switching on a light

Adding nothing new, only making visible what was already there.

Put together, ONUA means people who switch on a light with warmth.

The question

The question we began with

“Could design be shared freely, in the spirit of Korean jeong (情)?”

Around us were bowls shaped with years of care, songs recorded in small rooms, and shops that had held their corner for decades. The work was good enough. What it lacked was a name or a face to explain it, and so most of it stayed unknown.

Jeong is care given without keeping count. It is not pity and it is not a transaction; it is closer to the bond between people who look out for each other. That is the spirit in which ONUA shares design.

Pro bono branding

Why we work for free, and how it works

Branding is often the first thing to be postponed because of cost. Yet the smaller the practice, the more a name and a face can change. ONUA works by sharing time and conversation instead of charging fees.

  1. Receiving a story

    Stories reach us through the contact form or an introduction. Sometimes we go and find people ourselves.

  2. First conversation

    We listen to the work, the person and what is needed now, and talk honestly about timing and scope.

  3. Discovery & strategy

    We note the habits, materials and words already in the work, and decide together what to show first, and to whom.

  4. Design & story

    We make the name, identity, print and words, test them where they will actually be used, and refine them together.

  5. Handover & next chapter

    You receive editable files and a short guide, and we keep in touch for the steps that follow.

We can only take on a few projects at a time. When we can’t start right away, we’ll tell you why and when we might.
